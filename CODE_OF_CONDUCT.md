# Code of conduct

ast-pack follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be respectful and constructive, assume good intent, welcome newcomers, and keep discussion focused on the work. Harassment, personal attacks and discriminatory language are not tolerated.

Report unacceptable behaviour to the maintainer, Azimxon Vaxobov ([Telegram](https://t.me/A_Vakhobov), [LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/in/azimxon-vaxobov/)). Reports are handled confidentially.
